UX & Evidence Flow

How an operator moves through the product, and how a single suspicious dollar becomes a signed, re-verifiable proof. Two artboards: the task flow (what the human does) and the evidence pipeline (how the system decides — and where it refuses to).
The one rule the whole design protects: OCR is the only probabilistic step. Every money decision after it — arithmetic, grounding, price, industry benchmark, policy — is deterministic, traceable, and fail-closed. No model decides money; a person clears every flag. The receipt is the product.

Top nav + left rail are persistent; the operator can jump anywhere. The arrows show the intended daily path — triage, then act.

2Evidence pipeline — how a dollar is proven

Top-to-bottom is the deterministic pipeline. Every gate that isn't satisfied fails closed — it emits a named reason into the human Review Queue on the right, never a silent pass.
